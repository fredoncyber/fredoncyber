## About

I am a cybersecurity and risk-management practitioner with a background in military leadership, regulated manufacturing, and operational process improvement. My GitHub portfolio explores how security controls can become **measurable, testable, and repeatable engineering workflows**.

My areas of focus include governance, risk, and compliance (GRC); IT/OT security; critical infrastructure; cloud security; security automation; and infrastructure operations. I am pursuing a master's degree in cybersecurity and applying that learning through practical, documented projects.

> **Portfolio approach:** Define the problem → design the control or workflow → collect and validate evidence → document findings → improve and repeat.

## Working principles

- **Evidence over assertion:** Clearly distinguish implementations, prototypes, documented concepts, and planned work.
- **Security with operations in mind:** Consider availability, safety, maintainability, and recovery alongside confidentiality and integrity.
- **Reproducibility:** Prefer version-controlled procedures, schemas, tests, and traceable decisions.
- **Continuous improvement:** Turn gaps and feedback into actionable engineering changes.
